# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, respect differing viewpoints, and focus feedback on code and ideas rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation.
